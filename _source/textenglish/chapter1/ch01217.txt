On this earth the jivas are kings, citizens, etc. but if Indra does not send the rains, they will all die. And that Indra is insignificant before Brahmā, Vishnu and Shiv. And they are insignificant before Vairat. That Vairat is insignificant before Pradhan-Purush. And all these pale into insignificance before Akshar. And above that Akshar is Purushottam, whom we have attained in person today. Therefore, be strong due to one’s close association with him.$Supreme God (42.3) / (1/217)
