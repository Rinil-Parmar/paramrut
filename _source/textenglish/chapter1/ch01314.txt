If one has firm faith in God and his great Sadhu, one feels fulfilled and has no desire for any other company. For example, one who has 1000 million kilos of grains or a 1000 million rupees is not afraid of death if famine strikes. Another example, if one is protected by 2000 armed soldiers one is not afraid of being looted. Similarly, one who has firm faith coupled with the knowledge of God’s greatness has no fear.$Firm Faith in the Divine Form of God (43.8) / (1/314)
