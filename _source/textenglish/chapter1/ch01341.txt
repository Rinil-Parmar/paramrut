An aspirant engages in discourses, sings bhajans and talks about God, but does not believe, “I am not this body.” Therefore, 24 hours-a-day remember, “I am not the body, but I am the ātmā, who lives in the body. I am brahman, akshar and within me Paramatma Parameshwar Purushottam himself is eternally present.” What is he like? He is the cause of all avatārs, the cause of all causes and is above all. And his manifest form is the one I have attained. In this statement, both Sānkhya and Yoga are incorporated.$Atmanishtha-Brahmarup (29.10) / (1/341)
