Vachanamrut Gadhada III-13 was read and then Swami talked on the question, “How can one remain God-centred even when circumstances become very bad?” Swami said, “If complete faith remains, that is itself a characteristic of one who is God-centred. Just as, if the chintāmani remains and all other wealth is lost, then nothing is lost; but if the chintāmani is lost and all wealth remains, then nothing remains; similarly, if firm faith remains then everything remains and in the end only that will remain and is the means of final moksha.$Firm Faith in the Divine Form of God (43.3) / (1/203)
