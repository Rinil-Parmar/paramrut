A man cannot develop the same type of love he has for a woman toward God. He can develop love for God because of gnān though. And Maharaj used to say, “Krupanand Swami’s and Nishkulanand Swami’s love was [like that of a woman].”(1/330) @1. Nishkulanand Swami and Krupanand Swami possessed natural love for Maharaj because of their past sanskārs. Moreover, their love for Maharaj was like that of a faithful wife (pativratā).
