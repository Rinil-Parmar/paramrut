It is said in the last shloka of the Shikshapatri that is read daily:{Slok}Nijāshritānām sakalārtihantā, sadharma-bhakteravanam vidhātā;{Slok}Dātā sukhānām manasepsitānām tanotu kṛuṣhṇo-khila-mangalam naha.May God, destroyer of all miseries of his followers, protector of bhakti coupled with dharma, and bestower of all desired happiness to his devotees, bestow his devotees with prosperity.(1/333)
