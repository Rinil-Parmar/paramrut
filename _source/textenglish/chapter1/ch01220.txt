One who has gained the knowledge of God and this Sadhu has nothing left to do. Even though he is here, he is sitting in Akshardham. Therefore, one should not worry whether they turned five more or five less mālās. One should behave according to their ability. But one should keep God and this Sadhu in their jiva. Our greatness is not based on the spiritual endeavors; our greatness is based on the understanding of upāsanā.(1/220)
