When conflict first started between Duryodhan and the Pandavs, all the demons approached Duryodhan and said, “We will influence Kripacharya, Dronacharya, Bhishma Pita and others, so go to war.” The message from this is that when faults such as lust, anger, etc. enter into us, faults are attributed to the great, and so one may do what one should not (improper things may be done). At such times, realize that, “I have been influenced by demons and that (in reality) I am not like this.”$Base Instincts-Worldly Desires-Drawbacks-Sin (5.4) / (1/62)
