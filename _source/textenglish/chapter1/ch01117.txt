The great Sadhu gives more happiness to some and less to others. How should this be understood? In reply, he said, “The great are like an ocean, in which there is no shortage of water. The Sadhu does not give less happiness to people, but it appears that way to them due to the suitability of the recipient. Also, senior people have to be looked after – and this is not necessary for the meek. That is the way of the world. Meek people, like Parvatbhai, sit at the back and dignitaries sit at the front of the assembly.”$Mercy-Compassion-Grace of God (39.5) / (1/117)
