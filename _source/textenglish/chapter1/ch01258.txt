{Slok}[‘Shvān shūkar bilāḍ khar, tenā ṭoḷā māhyalo jant;{Slok}Tene mūke karī sant, jo maḷe Sadguru Sant.’]Swami spoke this proverb and said, “There is nothing like the Sadhu. We have attained that Sadhu.”(1/258)@ 1. Even if the jivas like dogs, pigs, cats, and donkeys come into contact of a Satpurush who is Aksharbrahman, then he can make them brahmarup. This proverb is mentioned in ‘Hariguru Sant’ by Akhā.
