Today we are happy because we have the company of the great (Sadhu). But if circumstances change and this company does not remain, how can we still remain happy? The answer to this question, “Think of the glory of the great (Sadhu): his virtues, personality, powers, influence, thoughtfulness, patience, etc. And thoughts of the great will spring up in one’s heart, as a result of which one will be happy.”Happiness (1.9) / (1/326)
