A devotee is able to maintain one of the three types of meditation: meditation in the form of conviction in God’s divine form, in the form of the desire to be in the company of the Sadhu or in the form of the belief that ‘I am a devotee of God’. Otherwise, to maintain uninterrupted concentration on the murti of God is possible only for sadhus such as Swarupanand Swami and others.$Qualities and Glory of a Devotee (21.6) / (1/282)
