No one knows the one responsible for everything that happens. Yet, in the midst of all that happens, others have [so many (doubtful) thoughts].(1/249) @1. Swami’s purport is to draw attention to the realization that God and the Sadhu know everything in Satsang. Specifically, they know everyone’s virtues and vices, good and bad actions, and good and bad intentions. Nothing is outside of their supervision. Not realizing this, aspirants in Satsang question why God and the Sadhu do not rebuke others who cause spoilage in Satsang or halt the growth of Satsang. Such are the thoughts of perceiving God and the Sadhu as human. However, one should realize that they know everything and allow certain things to happen; it is simply their divine action. There is no need to interfere with their divine actions.
