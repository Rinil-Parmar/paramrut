When faults (e.g. greed, anger, etc.) remain and are not overcome, does it mean they are merely faults or is there some virtue even in them? That is the question. The answer, “Faults hurt, so one remains meek and humble in Satsang, one retains a need for Satsang and one prays to God. And if one experiences difficulty due to faults, knowledge develops. Without it, such a need does not arise. Therefore, this is a virtue (in a fault).”$Base Instincts-Worldly Desires-Drawbacks-Sin (5.2) / (1/12)
