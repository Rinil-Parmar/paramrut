Just as one is absolutely convinced of one’s community, name and village, similarly, if one repeats, ‘I am ātmā, brahma, blissful and a devotee of God, but I am not this body,’ then this too can be learnt. And this body daily gives us an experience of hell, so what can be worse than that? But without spiritual knowledge we do not realize this.$Spiritual Wisdom-Understanding-Ignorance (26.7) / (1/98)
