Who can the Sun be compared with? It is one of a kind. Similarly, God is also one of a kind. And the countless avatārs have all attained the powers given by this one God. This opportunity has not arisen on earth before and will not come again. Even this Sadhu has not come on earth and will not come again, and this God has not come and will not come again. There have been infinite avatārs and there will be countless more avatārs, but this Sadhu and God have not come before and will not come again.$Supreme God (42.2) / (1/161)
