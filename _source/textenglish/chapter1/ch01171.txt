For one who performs (an Ashwamedh) yagna and rides a horse throughout the world it is very difficult, since if someone captures the horse the yagna will remain incomplete. While another, who rides the horse in the compound, completes the yagna. The meaning of this is, “Controlling the senses and inner faculties is like riding the horse throughout the world. While, believing oneself as brahmarup is like riding the horse in the compound. Also, imbibing the 64 qualities of a sadhu is like riding the horse throughout the world. But, associating with a sadhu who has the [64 qualities] is convenient, like riding the horse in the compound.”Atmanishtha-Brahmarup (29.3) / (1/171) @1.
