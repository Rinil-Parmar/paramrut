We should believe our true form to be akshar. If that cannot be believed, even then we should not believe the physical body as our true form. And Maharaj’s view was not to believe in the three bodies (as our own) and to believe ourselves as akshar. This is like a person born to a Brahmin family is a Brahmin, similarly, as we have attained God, we should believe our self as akshar.$Atmanishtha-Brahmarup (29.6) / (1/256)
