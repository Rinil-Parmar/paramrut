Where there is the great Sadhu, moral codes are observed, dharma is practised and spiritual wisdom is attained. Also, where there is the Sadhu there are infinite virtues, and also God. So, as a result, the jiva is purified. In the Vachanamrut, it is noted that God has said, “I am not as pleased by austerities, renunciation, yoga, observance of vows, donations or other endeavours as I am by the association of a Sadhu of complete inner purity. Having attained this satsang, the merits are limitless. Ajamil was a grave sinner, but he met Sanakadik, bowed to them and said, ‘I will not be able to do anything.’ But sadhus are compassionate, so they named his son Narayan, and in this way he attained moksha.”$Satsang (18.7) / (1/181)
