Until today we have run workshops to build mandirs, etc. and now we want to give such spiritual knowledge that one will never waver. Then Swami said, “See the happiness of all (animals, man, gods, etc.) and know the form of all different types of happiness; but apart from this God, there is no value in any other god. Learn this spiritual wisdom.” Then Swami said, “We want to uproot the Girnar-like lust, Meru-like ego and Lokalok-like strong worldly desires. We want to give such spiritual wisdom.”$Introspection (11.1) / (1/320)
