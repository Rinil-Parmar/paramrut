Darshan of this Sadhu destroys even the [five grave sins], but such glory is not realized. If one has realized such glory, fountains of joy will erupt from within.Are these merely talks? They are the talks of Akshardham. But without the knowledge of Sānkhya drawbacks remain. And to followers of the Sānkhya school of philosophy this world appears hellish. They do not believe it to have any value. In fact, to them all these workshops are of dust, therefore, do not believe them to be of any worth.$The Knowledge of Sankhya (30.15) / (1/127)@1.
