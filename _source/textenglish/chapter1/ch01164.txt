Vicious natures such as lust, anger, etc. are like pimples and ringworm! They are consequences of having a body. But they will be conquered. And if the great Sadhu showers his blessings, they will be overcome this instant. But to perceive flaws in the great Sadhu is like tuberculosis.$Perceiving the Virtues and Faults of Others (22.1) / (1/164)
