Maharaj used to say, “Let me describe my virtues and drawbacks. All the jivas of the universe believe in me, yet it has not given rise to ego in me. That is my virtue. And I send countless jivas into samādhi, but do not place Muktanand Swami and Gopalanand Swami in samādhi. That is my drawback.”$Grandeur of God (38.6) / (1/275)
