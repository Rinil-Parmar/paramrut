But understand that this is the power (i.e. to grant liberation instantly) of the company of the manifest form of God or his Sadhu as described in the scriptures.$The Manifest Form of God (46.1) / (1/260)@1. A powerful king of the Surya dynasty who fought on the side of the deities in their battle against the demons. When he realized he was soon to die, he sat in one of the heavenly planes of the deities and quickly went to Ayodhya to sit on the banks of the river Saryu. There, he meditated and gave up all attachments to his body, family, kingdom and wealth, and focused on God only. - Shrimad Bhagvat 9/9/41-49
