In our fellowship a person renounces worldly ties and becomes a renunciant because he has love for God and so is drawn to him. This is Yoga, but not Sānkhya. There may be much love for God but that is not Sānkhya. And if a practitioner of Sānkhya comes to become a sadhu and is told that he will only be made a sadhu if he goes and burns his family who is at home, then it is not difficult for him to do so. But one with mere Yoga cannot do this.$The Knowledge of Sankhya (27.1) / (1/86)
