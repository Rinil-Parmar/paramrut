Jhinabhai asked Maharaj to build a mandir in Junagadh. This must have been inspired by Maharaj. So he built a mandir here and kept a Sadhu like himself here. And ever since, we have not needed [to raise money].(1/273) @1. Gunatitanand Swami focused more on increasing faith rather than raising money. He knew that when faith increases, the haribhaktas will surrender their whole and soul for the mandir. Therefore, he never spoke about giving money to the mandir.
