65. ‘Hun’ tattva koṇ hashe? ‘hun’ e bhayankar ajnyān chhe. ‘Hun guṇātīt j chhun. Hun shuddha brahmarūp chhun, mārā indriyo-anta:karaṇ badhun j bhagavānanun.’ E jātanun bhajan have sharū karo. Jeṭalī chintā chhe eṭalī niṣhṭhā nathī. Āpaṇān indriyo-anta:karaṇ bhagavānamān virām karāvavānān chhe. To pachhī bījī kyān māthākūṭ karavānī rahī? Āpaṇe to sahej bhīḍo paḍe to ‘o bāp re’ thaī jāya. Parvatabhāī jevun apamān thāya to āpaṇī kevī sthiti rahe? E āpaṇe vichāravānun chhe.
