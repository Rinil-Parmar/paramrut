(Maharaj) has said in Vachanamrut Loya 7that, when one has reached the realization of gnān through the indriyas, antahkaran, and experience, then one can be called a complete gnāni. Regarding this, Swami said, “We have reached everything. But we do not realize this because [it is his wish].”(1/83)@ 1. Swami states that because we have attained God in the form of the Satpurush, we can realize God through all three: indriyas - through sight, touch, hearing, etc.; antahkaran - by contemplating and meditating; and experience - by experiencing bliss within.
