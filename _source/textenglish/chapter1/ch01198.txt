To conduct the activities of the whole universe in this workshop, and yet remain unattached is a feat only Sahajanand Swami can perform. What is this work like? It is like a 1000 kilo weight falling vertically on a lightweight mouse which then ceases to be seen. Thus, Maharaj said, “Well, whether or not even Gopalanand Swami and Muktanand Swami would remain like even a lowly person (by their involvement in worldly activities) is not guaranteed.” Therefore, we should only keep the company of God and his Sadhu. Since, no one is like them.$Grandeur of God (38.5) / (1/198)
