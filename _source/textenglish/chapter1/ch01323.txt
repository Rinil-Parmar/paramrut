Maharaj’s wish is to encourage only spiritual discourses, devotional songs, spiritual discussions and meditation. And man has developed such a nature that he cannot live without doing other mundane things. So, now, what should we do? We have this association (with the great Sadhu), but if a person does not understand and shed his base instincts and join with the great Sadhu, then he will go to Akshardham late. In this, God is in no hurry and these worldly activities will increase daily.$Base Instincts-Worldly Desires-Drawbacks-Sin (5.12) / (1/323)
