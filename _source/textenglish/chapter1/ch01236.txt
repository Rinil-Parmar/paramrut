These talks cannot be grasped even in a hundred lives, therefore all should keep these talks in mind. And attach the jiva (i.e. have close association) with two good sadhus and three good devotees so that one does not fall from satsang. And if traces of lust and greed still remain, do not worry. Maharaj has said in the Vachanamrut, “Even if one has no desires of lust and greed, but if one’s jiva is not attached to the enlightened Sadhu of God, what is the use? In the absence of this attachment one will become demonic.”$Profound Association-Love-Devotion-Friendship (19.5) / (1/236)
