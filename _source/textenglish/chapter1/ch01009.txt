One who renounces worldly life and becomes a renunciant overcomes all miseries and becomes happy. But even after becoming a renunciant, the misery associated with base instincts – greed, lust, taste, attachments and ego – remains. As these base instincts are (gradually) overcome, one becomes happier.$Base Instincts-Worldly Desires-Drawbacks-Sin (5.1) / (1/9)
