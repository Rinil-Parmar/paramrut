Today, God has come to this earth with his Akshardham (Aksharbrahman incarnate). That the power of his human form cannot be understood is a grave sin. Thus, do not be like the Yadavs who did not understand the glory of Shri Krishna, but become a devotee like Uddhavji.To call the great Sadhu as being like others or inferior to others amounts to insulting him. And never before have there been such discourses and understanding as there is today.$Supremacy of Akshardham (36.2) / (1/221)
