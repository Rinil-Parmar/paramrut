Prahladji fought for many days with Narayan, but God was not won over. Then God told Prahlad, “I cannot be won over by such wars. The way to win me over is by singing my bhajans, thinking of me in your mind and cherishing my murti in your eyes. In this way, always remember me.” Then, Prahlad tried this method and God was won over within six months. Thus, to please God, learn this method, which is the best.$To Please God and His Holy Sadhu (34.1) / (1/3)
