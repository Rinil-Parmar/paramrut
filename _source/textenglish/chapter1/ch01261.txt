After reading Vachanamrut Gadhada II-9, Swami said, “If one knows Maharaj as Purushottam, but, due to circumstances, leaves Satsang, even then one attains Akshardham. And if one does not have this spiritual wisdom to recognize Maharaj as Purushottam but remains in Satsang, then he will attain another abode, but not Akshardham.”$Supreme God (42.4) / (1/261)
