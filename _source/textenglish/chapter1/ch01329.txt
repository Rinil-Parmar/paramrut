God has called the spiritually wise his ātmā. Uddhav was spiritually wise. God will certainly care for those with affection, but without spiritual knowledge one is incomplete. When Sachchidanand Swami was thirsty, Maharaj’s thirst could not be quenched. Then, when Sachchidanand Swami was served water Maharaj’s thirst was quenched. Still Maharaj’s opinion is that one should believe one’s ātmā as brahmarup.$Atmanishtha-Brahmarup (29.8) / (1/329)
