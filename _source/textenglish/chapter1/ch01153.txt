Today, there is no mantra more powerful than the Swaminarayan mantra. It makes even the poison of a black cobra ineffective and dispels the desire for material pleasures. With it, one becomes brahmarup and is freed from the bondage of kāl, karma and māyā. That is how powerful this mantra is. Therefore, always chant it.$Glory of the Swaminarayan Mahamantra (49.2) / (1/153)
