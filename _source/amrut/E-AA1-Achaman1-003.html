3. Āsharāno bījo artha : nishchintatā-nirbhayatā-akhanḍa rakṣhā. Dṛuḍh āsharo rākhīe to hāsye sahit pūrṇatāe pahonchāya. Je thavānun hoya te thāya, paṇ āsharo chhoḍavo nahi. Je mūnzāya chhe, kalesh āve chhe ene āsharāmān pol chhe.
Mūnzavaṇ shun? Koīno abhāv āve, māna-apamāna, sukha-du:khamān bhāvafer thāya e mūnzavaṇa. Koī gamatun mukāve tyāre mūnzavaṇ thāya, bhīḍāmān le tyāre mūnzavaṇ thāya, gam khāya tyāre mūnzavaṇ thāya. ‘tun rājī thāya chhe ne !’ basa, emān atūṭ shraddhā rākhavī.
