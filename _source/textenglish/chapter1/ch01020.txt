Only God and his holy Sadhu can grant moksha. Detachment cultivates enmity towards material pleasures but does not do the job of God; ātmā-realization breaks attachment from everything but does not do the job of God; and by dharma one remains happy but it also does not do the job of God. Thus, only God and his holy Sadhu give moksha. Therefore, do not find faults in them.$Glory of the Sadhu (30.5) / (1/20)
