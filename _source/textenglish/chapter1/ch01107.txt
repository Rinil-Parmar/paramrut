Sulabhā could experience samādhi and was able to enter another’s body. However, when she went to the forest and saw the beauty, she thought, “If there was a man, [we could play].”(1/107) @1. Here, Swami gives a example of what he has mentioned in Swamini Vat 1/37: Even if one has the ability to experience samādhi at will, their desires to enjoy the vishays is not eradicated. Sulabhā was a woman who could experience samādhi, yet she still had desires to enjoy the vishays (i.e., desired the company of a man).
