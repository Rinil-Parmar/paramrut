Priyavrat had children; yet he was known as ‘ātmārām bhāgwat’ because he never abandoned the discourse of God and the Sadhu who is dear to God. Moreover, Janak said, “Nothing of mine is burning in the city of Mithilā.” Yet, he also had children. To Govardhanbhai, sugar and salt were the same; yet he had children. When one stays close (to a woman) (i.e., one is married), [they will have children].(1/113)@ 1. Swami’s purport here is that a gruhasth who has reached an elevated state may engage in their familial duties and have children, despite being aloof of such activities. Swami has praised Priyavat in other Swamini Vatos because he had a zeal for listening to discourses of God. Janak understood everything in this world as false. Gordhanbhai had conquered taste.
