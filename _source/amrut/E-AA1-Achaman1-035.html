35. Āpaṇane mahārājano sambandha chhe to paṇ āpaṇane be chīj naḍe chhe : āḷas ane sanga. Pahelī āḷas naḍe. Bījī beṭhaka-ūṭhak naḍe. Āḷasathī shun karavānun chhe? E jāṇatā hoīe topaṇ nā thāya. Beṭhaka-ūṭhakathī ḍhasaḍāī javāya. Āḷas n hoya evā santo sāthe beṭhaka-ūṭhak ne maitrī hoya to mūnzāvā nā de. Jeno jīv atishaya baḷane pāmyo hoya te chovīs kalāk prabhumaya rahe. Āpaṇe e rītanā thavun chhe.
