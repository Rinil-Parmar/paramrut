In Vanthali Swami said, “If this Vanthali village is given as a gift to someone he would go mad. So what would be his condition if he is given Vadodara? We have attained tens of millions of [Vadodaras] – even that is an understatement. So now, as long as we live, eat simple food and worship God. And both God and the Sadhu wants to give us food, so they will give. When we die, we want to sit next to God. Our ātmā will go up while our body, like a discarded shirt, will lie here.”$Glory of Attainment (32.8) / (1/215)@1.
