During a reading of the scriptures, it was mentioned that Vyasji did not attain peace. Explaining this, Swami said, “We have attained God and the God-like Sadhu, still we do not feel at peace. The reasons: first, a strong desire for worldly pleasures; second, inability to act according to the commands of God; and third, ignorance. Because of these, peace is not experienced.”$Misery (2.3) / (1/159)
