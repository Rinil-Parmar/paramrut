Innate desires (of the jiva to enjoy worldly pleasures) are not burnt even by the fire of the Great Dissolution. Innate desire is māyā in the form of the causal body. The means to overcome it is, first, the upāsanā of God and, second, the observance of God’s commands – that is to act as stated in the Shikshapatri (verse 116): Nijātmānam brahmarupam. With these words, Maharaj has made everyone become God-realized.$Upasana (40.1) / (1/130)
