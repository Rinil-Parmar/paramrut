“When one overcomes all desires (of enjoying vishays), then one would not like this world or their body. And they would be pained having to live in this world.” Thus, Swami spoke. Someone asked a question regarding this, “What should one do about the pain experienced because one still has desires?” Swami answered, “That pain is beneficial, because it allows one to remain humble. Whatever God does is appropriate. And [Maharaj] revealed the form of the body to Sura Khachar in the village Faneni by showing him the form of the ear. This caused Sura Khachar to vomit. If others saw similarly, the same would happen to them.”(1/79)
