[Maharaj has already eradicated our flaws.] And that we realize our faults is for our own benefit, otherwise, the jiva would become impudent. Now, all our efforts are to win God’s favor and to attain powers like his.(1/58) @1. With these words, Swami is giving aspirants strength to not lose courage when seeing their faults. God and the Satpurush have the power to eradicate one’s vices. However, one should think of the attainment of God and continue to fight their internal enemies when they arise.
